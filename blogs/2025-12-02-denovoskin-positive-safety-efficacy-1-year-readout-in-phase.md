CUTISS is pleased to share that the 1-year results of the Phase IIb clinical trial evaluating denovoSkin™ in adolescent and adult burn patients have been published in eClinicalMedicine, part of The Lancet Discovery Science. The prospective, randomized, controlled multicenter study compared denovoSkin™ to split-thickness skin grafts, the current surgical standard. The efficacy readout shows that denovoSkin™ demonstrated a markedly higher expansion capacity from a small biopsy, and a minimally scarring outcome, in sharp contrast to the intra-patient control.
