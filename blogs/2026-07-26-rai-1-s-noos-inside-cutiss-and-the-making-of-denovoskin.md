Italian national broadcaster Rai 1 visited CUTISS for a feature on denovoSkin™ as part of Noos – L’avventura della conoscenza, the popular science programme hosted by Alberto Angela. The reportage takes viewers inside CUTISS’ GMP manufacturing facility in Schlieren to explore how denovoSkin™, the personalized, bio-engineered skin graft, is produced from a small sample of a patient’s own healthy skin.
