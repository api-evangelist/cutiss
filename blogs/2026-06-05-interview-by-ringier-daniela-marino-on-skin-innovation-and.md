CEO and co-founder Daniela Marino is featured in the summer 2026 edition of Interview by Ringier, the Swiss magazine dedicated to in-depth conversations with personalities from business, science, culture, politics and sport. In the four-page interview, titled “Zweite Haut” (“Second Skin”), Daniela discusses the importance and complexity of human skin, the challenges faced by patients with severe burns, and how CUTISS is developing bio-engineered skin tissue to support both healing and quality of life. She explains how denovoSkin™ is produced from a small biopsy of a patient’s own healthy skin
